# TechStore Return, Refund, Exchange & Warranty Policy

## 1. General Return Policy

TechStore accepts returns for eligible products within **30 calendar days of purchase**.

The return period begins on the purchase date shown on the original order confirmation.

To be eligible for a return:

* The product must be unused.
* The product must be in its original packaging.
* All original accessories, manuals, and included components must be returned.
* The product must not show signs of misuse or intentional damage.
* A valid order ID or proof of purchase may be required.

Products that do not meet these conditions may not be eligible for return.

## 2. Damaged Products

Products damaged during delivery must be reported within **7 calendar days of delivery**.

Customers reporting a damaged product should provide:

* Order ID
* Description of the damage
* Photographs of the damaged product
* Photographs of the packaging, when applicable
* Delivery date, when available

The **7-day damaged-product reporting period is separate from the standard 30-day return period**.

Reporting damage after the 7-day period does not automatically extend the damaged-product reporting period.

Additional information may be requested during the damage assessment.

## 3. Defective Products

Products that develop a functional defect may be handled under the applicable warranty or defective-product process.

Customers should provide:

* Order ID
* Description of the defect
* Details about when the problem was first noticed
* Any troubleshooting information requested by TechStore

Defective products are not necessarily subject to the same process or timeframe as products damaged during delivery.

## 4. Refunds

Refunds are issued after the applicable return or refund request has been reviewed and approved.

Once a refund is approved, it is **processed within 5 business days**.

The refund processing period begins after refund approval.

The 5-business-day processing period does not begin automatically from the original purchase date or from the date the return request was submitted.

The time required for the refunded amount to appear in the customer's bank account or payment method may vary.

## 5. Refund Eligibility

A return request and a refund are separate stages of the return process.

The typical process is:

1. Return request submitted
2. Return reviewed
3. Return approved
4. Refund approved
5. Refund processed

Being within the 30-day return period does not guarantee that a refund will be approved.

The **30-day return period and 5-business-day refund processing period are separate timeframes**.

They should not be treated as a guaranteed 35-day period from the original purchase date.

## 6. Exchanges

Eligible products may be exchanged when an exchange option is available.

Exchange eligibility depends on:

* Product condition
* Availability of the replacement product
* Applicable return conditions
* Product-specific restrictions

An exchange is not guaranteed for every returned product.

If an exchange is unavailable, the customer may request a refund where applicable.

## 7. Warranty

Warranty coverage depends on the individual product and its applicable warranty terms.

The standard **30-day return period is separate from warranty coverage**.

A product may have warranty coverage beyond the standard return period.

Warranty duration and coverage may vary by product, manufacturer, and product category.

The return policy does not define one universal warranty period for all TechStore products.

## 8. Return Shipping

Customers must follow the return shipping instructions provided by TechStore for an approved return.

Return shipping responsibility may depend on the reason for the return and the applicable return instructions.

Return shipping is not automatically free for every type of return.

Additional shipping instructions may be provided during the return process.

## 9. Non-Returnable Products and Conditions

A return may not be accepted when:

* The applicable return period has expired.
* The product has been used in a manner inconsistent with its intended purpose.
* The product has been intentionally damaged.
* Required accessories or components are missing.
* The product cannot be verified as an eligible purchase.
* The return does not satisfy the applicable product conditions.

Certain products may also have product-specific return restrictions.

## 10. Important Policy Timeframes

| Policy Area                      | Timeframe                                       |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Standard product return          | Within 30 calendar days of purchase             |
| Damaged product reporting        | Within 7 calendar days of delivery              |
| Refund processing after approval | Within 5 business days                          |
| Warranty coverage                | Varies by product and applicable warranty terms |

These timeframes apply to different stages of the customer service process.

The timeframes should not automatically be combined to calculate a single overall return or refund duration.

## 11. Policy Clarifications

The following situations depend on the applicable product, order, or return conditions:

* Exact time for a refund to appear in a customer's bank account
* Specific warranty duration for an individual product
* Availability of a replacement product
* Responsibility for return shipping
* Eligibility of products with missing accessories
* Eligibility of products with signs of use or damage

Product-specific terms may provide additional conditions that apply to a particular purchase.
